# An Incoherence Adventure

Once upon a time, a king lived in a castle on a hill.

![castle](img/castle.png)

One day, a huge, fire-breathing dragon landed right in front of the gate and blocked the entrance.

![dragon](img/dragon.png)

The knight rushed to report the news to the king. Meanwhile, the scientist and the wise wizard quickly opened an ancient map and said, "The only way to calm the dragon is to find the magic Moonstone hidden deep in the forest."

![scientist](img/scientist.png)

![wizard](img/wizard.png)

![map](img/map.png)

Suddenly, a pirate burst into the room and yelled, "Walking through the forest takes too long! I have a faster way—we should take a rocket!"

![pirate](img/pirate.png)

Everyone agreed, climbed into the rocket, and blasted into the sky.

![rocket](img/rocket.png)

When they landed safely in the forest, the sharp-eyed detective spotted a spooky ghost hiding behind the trees.

![detective](img/detective.png)

![ghost](img/ghost.png)

As they ventured deeper into the woods, a giant ogre stomped over and blocked their path! Just as everyone froze in fear, a clever little cat walked up to the ogre, purred loudly, and rubbed against his legs. The soft-hearted ogre smiled and let them pass.

![orge](img/ogre.png)

![cat](img/cat.png)

Near a broken stone gate, they finally found a lonely alien holding a small treasure box.

![alien](img/alien.png)

![treasure_small](img/treasure_small.png)

The alien apologized nervously, "I didn't mean to steal it! I thought this small box was a battery to power my ship. The real key to the big chest is inside!"

After forgiving the alien, he unlocked the box and guided the team to the hidden main treasure chest.

![treasure_open](img/treasure_open.png)

Inside, the Moonstone glowed brilliantly. The knight cheered, the kingdom was safe again, and everyone shared the treasure happily ever after.

![knight](img/knight.png)

![treasure_large](img/treasure_large.png)
